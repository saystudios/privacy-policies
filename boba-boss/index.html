Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Say Studios LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) makes Boba Boss (the “App”). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Information We Collect

None. The App does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ins.

Game Progress

Your progress (coins, stars, unlocked days, upgrades, helpers and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the App deletes this saved progress.

No Chat, Ads or Tracking

The App has no chat, social features or third-party advertising, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. It contains no analytics or advertising software.

Purchases

The first 5 days of the App are free. One optional in-app purchase, the “Full Café,” unlocks the rest of the game. Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store using your Apple Account, and Apple handles all payment details; we never receive your name, card or payment information. A grown-ups-only question appears before any purchase or restore, so children can’t buy by accident. Apple tells the App whether the purchase was made so it can unlock the café, and that is stored only on your device.

Children’s Privacy

Boba Boss is designed to be family-friendly and is suitable for children. Because the App collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children.

Information Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Policy Updates

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
