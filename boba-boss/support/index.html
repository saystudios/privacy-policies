Common Questions

How do I play?

Tap a guest to take their order, then build the drink at the counter: pick a tea, add milk or fruit, add toppings (and ice if they want it), then tap the sealer to shake it. Tap the guest again to serve. Earn enough coins before the clock runs out to pass the day. Tap How to play on the title screen any time for a refresher.

What do I get with the Full Café?

Days 1–5 are free. The one-time Full Café purchase unlocks Days 6–30, the Sunny Boardwalk and Downtown Plaza shops, every drink and topping, and special visits. It's a single purchase: no subscription and no ads.

I bought the Full Café but it's locked. How do I restore it?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Then open 👪 Parent corner on the title screen (or tap a locked day) and choose Restore purchase. A grown-up question appears first.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can my child buy things by accident?

No. A grown-ups-only math question appears before any purchase or restore. You can also turn off in-app purchases in Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.

What's in the Parent corner?

Tap 👪 on the title screen and answer the grown-up question. There you can turn sound effects, music and seasonal events (like Halloween) on or off, set a play-time reminder (15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes), restore your purchase, or reset progress. The play-time reminder only appears at the end of a day or when the game is paused, never in the middle of making a drink.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or using Reset progress, erases it. It doesn't move to a new device. Your Full Café purchase is never lost: use Restore purchase to get it back.

Who is Karen, and what is Karen Rush?

Karen is a super picky (and very funny) guest who visits from Day 4. She has less patience, but pays extra if you make her drink perfectly! After Day 6, the map shows 🚨 Karen Rush, a bonus challenge with two Karens at once. Serve two of them to win bonus coins once a day.

What are stickers and the daily gift?

Stickers are collected by doing fun things in the café, like serving each signature drink or making Karen happy. See them in the 📒 Stickers book. Open the 🎁 Daily gift each day for free coins. Coming back several days in a row makes the gift bigger.

There's no sound.

Check that your device isn't on silent mode and the volume is up, then check the 🔊 and 🎵 buttons on the title screen or in the Parent corner.

The game looks small or cut off.

Boba Boss works in both directions, but the café looks biggest with your iPhone turned sideways. On iPad, it fills the screen either way.
