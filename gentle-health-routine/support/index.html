Common Questions

How do I turn on reminder notifications?

Open Settings in the App and tap Turn on notifications, then tap Allow. If you said no before, open your iPhone’s Settings → Gentle Health → Notifications and turn on Allow Notifications. Use Send a test notification in the App to check.

My reminders didn’t arrive.

Check that notifications are on (above) and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing them. The App sets up reminders for the next 7 days and refreshes them each time you open it, so open Gentle Health Routine at least once a week.

How do I use Done and Snooze on a notification?

Press and hold a reminder notification (or swipe down on it) to see Done and Snooze 15 min. Tapping either one opens Gentle Health Routine briefly and saves the change.

How do I add a medicine or blood sugar reminder?

Go to Settings → Reminders and notifications and tap Add medicine reminder or Add blood sugar check. Use the times and amounts your doctor or pharmacist gave you. These reminders are never added unless you add them.

How do I set a checkup reminder (A1C, eye exam, dental)?

In Settings → Reminders and notifications → Checkups, tap the checkup, then choose the date and time of your appointment. Upcoming checkups appear under Coming up. When you mark one done, the App offers to set the next one.

How do I add my plan and an emergency contact?

Go to Settings → My plan and emergency contact. Type the plan your doctor gave you, plus a name and phone number, and tap Save. A My plan card with a call button then appears on the Today screen.

How do I switch between English and Lao?

Tap the language button at the top of any screen, or go to Settings → Language.

Can the App read text aloud or make it bigger?

Yes. Tap Read aloud next to a reminder or tip. In Settings → Voice and reading, turn on Easy view for bigger text and buttons and a simpler Today screen (it’s on automatically for ages 65+). You can also choose a larger text size, high contrast and a slower reading speed. Read-aloud uses the voices on your device, and some devices may not have a Lao voice.

I tapped Done by mistake.

Tap Undo in the message at the bottom of the screen right after, or tap the check circle next to the reminder again.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your data is saved only on your device. In Settings → Privacy and backup, tap Copy backup and paste it somewhere safe, such as a note or an email to yourself. On the new device, paste it into Restore from backup.

My data disappeared.

Deleting the App erases its data. If you saved a backup, restore it in Settings → Privacy and backup.

Does Gentle Health Routine give medical advice?

No. Gentle Health Routine is a reminder and tracking tool. It does not diagnose conditions or change treatment. Always follow your doctor or care team.

Privacy: Gentle Health Routine has no accounts, ads or tracking, and your information stays on your device. Read the Privacy Policy.
