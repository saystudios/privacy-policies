Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Say Studios LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) makes Say Math Jr. (the “App”), a math learning app for children in Kindergarten through Grade 2. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Information We Collect

None. The App does not collect, store on our servers, or send anywhere any personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ins.

Player Name and Progress

When setting up, a child can type a first name or nickname and choose a buddy picture and grade. This, along with stars, stickers, the day streak, lesson results and settings, is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the App, or using “New player” in the Grown-ups area, erases it.

Read-Aloud Voice

The App can read questions out loud using the speech voice built into your iPhone or iPad. The speech is created on the device; no recordings are made and the App does not use the microphone or camera.

No Ads, Chat, Purchases or Tracking

The App has no advertising, no chat or social features, no in-app purchases and no links that leave the App. It does not track you across other apps or websites and contains no analytics or advertising software. It does not use the internet.

Children’s Privacy

Say Math Jr. is made for children. Because the App collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children, and it is designed to follow the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple’s rules for apps in the Kids category. Settings for grown-ups are behind a grown-ups-only question.

Information Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Policy Updates

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
