Common Questions

How does my child play?

Pick a game on the home screen, like Counting or Adding. Each game has 5 questions. Pip the puppy reads each question out loud. Tap an answer: a right answer gets a cheer, and a wrong one gets a gentle hint so your child can try again. Finishing a game earns 1 to 3 stars.

Which grades is it for?

Kindergarten, Grade 1 and Grade 2. Each grade sees games and numbers that fit it: for example, counting to 10 and adding with pictures in Kindergarten, up to two-digit adding, skip counting and telling time to 5 minutes in Grade 2.

How do I change the grade or name?

Tap the ⚙️ gear on the home screen and answer the grown-up question. In the Grown-ups area you can change the name and grade, turn the read-aloud voice and vibration on or off, and see how your child is doing in each game.

What do the stars and stickers mean?

Each game gives up to 3 stars (3 stars means no mistakes). Every 5 stars unlocks a new sticker in the 📒 Sticker Book. Playing on back-to-back days builds a 🔥 day streak.

There’s no voice.

Check that the volume is up, and that “Read questions out loud” is on in the Grown-ups area. Your child can also tap the 🔊 button to hear a question again.

Does it cost anything, or have ads?

No. Say Math Jr. has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

My child’s progress disappeared.

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or choosing “Reset stars and stickers” or “New player” in the Grown-ups area, erases it. It doesn’t move to a new device.
