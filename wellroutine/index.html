Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Say Studios LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) makes WellRoutine (the “App”), a daily reminder and tracking app for people living with diabetes. This policy explains how information is handled in the App on iPhone, iPad and the web.

The Short Version

We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information We Collect

None. The App has no accounts or sign-ins, and it does not send your information to us or to any server.

What the App Saves on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following only on your device:

  • Your language, age group, goals and display settings
  • Your reminders (including medicine, blood sugar and checkup reminders you choose to add) and which ones you completed, skipped or snoozed
  • Activity minutes, water, mood and notes
  • Blood sugar readings, if you turn on blood sugar tracking
  • The plan you type in from your doctor and your emergency contact’s name and phone number, if you add them
  • Meal plans, saved recipes, grocery lists and any food photos you add

We cannot see this information. Deleting the App deletes it.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled by iOS on your device. Tapping Done or Snooze on a notification updates the App on your device only. No notification content is sent over the internet.

Calling Your Emergency Contact

If you tap the call button, the App opens your phone’s Phone app with the number you entered. The App does not place, record or log calls.

Camera and Photos

The App uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a recipe. The photo is stored on your device and is not uploaded.

Backups

If you use Copy backup, the App copies your data as text to your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you. iOS device backups (iCloud or a computer) may include the App’s data under Apple’s own privacy policy.

No Ads, Analytics or Tracking

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Health Information

The App is a reminder and tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions or give treatment advice. Always follow the guidance of your doctor or care team.

Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Information Sharing

We do not share or sell information, because we do not receive any.

Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
